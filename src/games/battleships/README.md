# Battleships - Documentation

## Overview
Naval combat strategy game. Place your ships on a 10x10 grid and take turns with the AI to find and sink all enemy ships.

## Current Implementation (Level 1 - POC)
- ✅ 10x10 grid
- ✅ 5 ships: Carrier(5), Battleship(4), Cruiser(3), Submarine(3), Destroyer(2)
- ✅ Ship placement phase with rotation
- ✅ Turn-based gameplay
- ✅ Hit/miss feedback with visual indicators
- ✅ Ship sunk detection
- ✅ AI opponent (random shooting)
- ✅ Win/loss detection

## How to Play
**Placement Phase:**
1. Click "Rotate" to change ship orientation
2. Click on your board to place ships
3. All ships must be placed before battle

**Battle Phase:**
1. Click on enemy board to attack
2. Red = Hit, Gray = Miss
3. Sink all enemy ships to win!

## Expansion Levels
**Level 2**: Smart AI (hunt/target mode)
**Level 3**: Advanced AI with probability maps
**Level 4**: Salvo mode, special abilities
**Level 5**: Online multiplayer
